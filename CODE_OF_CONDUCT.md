# Knowledge Forge AI Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

Knowledge Forge AI is committed to maintaining project spaces where people can collaborate safely, professionally, and in good faith.

Participation is welcome regardless of background, identity, experience level, disability, neurodiversity, nationality, language, religion or philosophy, socioeconomic status, sex, gender, gender identity or expression, sexual orientation, or other personal characteristics unrelated to the work.

## Expected Behavior

Participants are expected to:

- engage respectfully and honestly;
- focus criticism on ideas, designs, code, evidence, and outcomes rather than people;
- respect differing technical judgments and lived experiences;
- give and receive constructive feedback in good faith;
- respect clearly stated boundaries;
- take responsibility for mistakes and make reasonable efforts to repair harm;
- protect private, confidential, security-sensitive, and personally identifying information; and
- help keep project spaces useful for engineering, documentation, design, research, and community collaboration.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following are not acceptable in Knowledge Forge AI project spaces:

- harassment, intimidation, threats, stalking, or sustained unwanted attention;
- insults, demeaning personal remarks, or attacks directed at an individual or group;
- discriminatory or exclusionary conduct based on personal characteristics;
- sexualized conduct or unwelcome sexual attention in project spaces;
- deliberate disruption of discussions, reviews, issue trackers, or development workflows;
- publishing another person's private information without permission;
- knowingly exposing credentials, secrets, private vulnerability details, or other security-sensitive information through public channels;
- retaliation against someone for making a good-faith conduct or security report; or
- other conduct that a reasonable person would consider abusive, threatening, or seriously inappropriate in a professional collaborative environment.

Disagreement, direct technical criticism, rejection of a proposal, or enforcement of project scope and contribution requirements is not by itself a conduct violation when handled professionally.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to Knowledge Forge AI repositories, issue trackers, pull requests, discussions, documentation spaces, and other organization-managed community spaces. It also applies when someone is acting as an official representative of Knowledge Forge AI or one of its projects.

Repository-specific codes of conduct may add project-local guidance. Where they conflict with this organization default, the repository-local policy governs that repository.

## Reporting Conduct Problems

Reports of harassment, abuse, threats, retaliation, or other conduct violations should be made privately to **lair001@gmail.com**.

Do not use a public issue, pull request, or discussion to disclose private information about the people involved. Include only the information reasonably needed to understand the incident, such as the relevant project or community space, what occurred, approximate timing, and links or records that are safe to share.

Security vulnerabilities should instead follow [SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md).

## Response and Enforcement

Knowledge Forge AI maintainers may take action reasonably proportionate to the conduct, context, evidence, impact, and recurrence. Actions may include:

1. a clarification, correction, or private warning;
2. removal or moderation of content;
3. temporary restrictions on participation;
4. rejection or closure of contributions or discussions; or
5. permanent exclusion from organization-managed community spaces for severe or repeated violations.

Maintainers may skip intermediate steps when the circumstances warrant it. Reports will be handled as confidentially as reasonably possible, subject to the need to investigate, protect participants, comply with law, or enforce project policies.

These enforcement guidelines do not limit GitHub's own terms, moderation, abuse-reporting systems, or enforcement authority.
