# Commercial Licensing

Knowledge Forge AI generally uses the GNU Affero General Public License v3.0 or later (`AGPL-3.0-or-later`) as its community software license, with separate commercial licensing available where an individual project states so.

## Repository terms control

Licensing is defined per repository. This organization-level document is a summary and does not replace or modify any project's `LICENSE`, `NOTICE`, `COMMERCIAL-LICENSE.md`, third-party notices, or other licensing files.

If a repository's licensing terms differ from this summary, the repository-specific terms control.

## AGPL community licensing

The AGPL permits commercial use subject to its terms. A separate commercial license is **not** merely permission to use software commercially.

Projects offering commercial licensing make it available for users who need proprietary or negotiated terms that the AGPL community license does not provide.

Commercial-license use cases may include:

- closed-source embedding;
- private SaaS deployments with proprietary modifications;
- OEM redistribution;
- proprietary integration into commercial products;
- support commitments;
- warranty or indemnity terms; and
- custom procurement, compliance, or contract terms.

## Contributions and dual licensing

Knowledge Forge AI contribution terms are structured to preserve the project steward's ability to distribute accepted contributions as part of the applicable project under `AGPL-3.0-or-later`, separate commercial licenses, and other future project licenses permitted by the applicable CLA.

See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) and [CLA.md](CLA.md) for the organization defaults. Repository-specific contribution terms override these defaults when present.
