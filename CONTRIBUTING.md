# Contributing to Knowledge Forge AI

Knowledge Forge AI welcomes contributions to its public projects under terms that preserve both open community distribution and the ability to offer separate commercial licenses.

## Scope and precedence

This document is the organization-default contribution policy for Knowledge Forge AI repositories that do not provide their own `CONTRIBUTING.md` or other repository-specific contribution terms.

If a repository provides its own `CONTRIBUTING.md`, `CLA.md`, pull request template, or other contribution policy, **the repository-local terms control**.

Contributions governed by this organization-default policy are accepted only under the terms in [CLA.md](CLA.md).

- GNU Affero General Public License v3.0 or later (`AGPL-3.0-or-later`);
- commercial licenses; and
- future project licenses adopted by the project steward, including open-source, source-available, and commercial licenses.

See [CLA.md](CLA.md) for the complete organization-default grant and conditions.

## Authority and third-party material

Contributions must be original work or submitted with authority from the relevant rights holder.

Do not submit code, documentation, generated output, data, media, or other material unless you have the right to provide it under the applicable contribution terms.

If a contribution contains third-party material:

- identify that material clearly;
- preserve all required copyright, attribution, license, and notice information;
- ensure its license is compatible with the applicable project; and
- do not represent third-party work as your own contribution.

## Pull request acknowledgment

Every pull request governed by the organization-default CLA must include this explicit acknowledgment in the pull request body:

```text
I agree to the Knowledge Forge AI contribution terms in CLA.md.
```

A `Signed-off-by` line may be included, but it does not replace the explicit acknowledgment. Contributions without the required acknowledgment cannot be accepted.

Pull requests should also confirm that the contributor has authority to submit the work under the applicable terms.

If the target repository contains its own `CLA.md` or pull request template, use the exact acknowledgment required there instead.

## Project quality requirements

Individual projects define their own technical, testing, documentation, compatibility, and review requirements. Follow the target repository's README, contribution documentation, development instructions, and pull request template.

## Licensing questions

Knowledge Forge AI generally uses `AGPL-3.0-or-later` as its community software license with separate commercial licensing available where a project states so. See the target repository's licensing files for authoritative terms.

Questions about commercial licensing or contribution terms can be sent to **lair001@gmail.com**.
