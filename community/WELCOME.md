## Find your corner of the forge

- **Agent engineering:** [APGR](https://www.knowledge-forge.ai/agentic-praxis-grimoire/)
  supplies bounded engineering primitives and skills.
- **Repository understanding:** [RepoMap, rebranding toward CLOE](https://github.com/Knowledge-Forge-AI/repo-map),
  develops evidence-linked repository knowledge graphs. [DINAS originally inspired
  the map](https://www.knowledge-forge.ai/journal/dinas-2026-10-22/).
- **Visual systems:** [Theme Forge](https://tfnf.knowledge-forge.ai/) connects SVG
  assets, Starlight and Tailwind/shadcn themes, and a desktop workbench. Both
  [STTN](https://sttn.knowledge-forge.ai/) and [CTTN](https://cttn.knowledge-forge.ai/)
  have their own sites and npm packages.
- **Distribution:** [RS9](https://rs9.knowledge-forge.ai/), the
  [Homebrew tap](https://brew.rs9.knowledge-forge.ai/) and
  [signed Linux repositories](https://tf.rs9.knowledge-forge.ai/) have distinct
  roles and project-specific qualification.
- **Workstations and test environments:** [DINAS](https://github.com/Knowledge-Forge-AI/darwin-is-not-a-snowflake)
  has public source previews; [ESA](https://github.com/Knowledge-Forge-AI/ephemeral-snowflake-artificer)
  develops image contracts and validation foundations.

## Ways to take part

Ask a question, share an installation experience, suggest a workflow improvement,
or show a theme, integration or documentation site you made. A small example,
screenshot or clear description of a confusing step is useful.

Use Discussions for exploration and feedback. Use a project’s issue tracker for
a reproducible bug or concrete work item, and link related discussions. Include
the project name, version/source revision and installation method when relevant.

Each project’s README and release records define its current maturity and support.
Released tools, pilots and source previews have different boundaries. Please keep
conversation respectful and follow the [Code of Conduct](https://github.com/Knowledge-Forge-AI/.github/blob/main/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
Code, documentation and asset contributions follow the target repository’s terms.
